COST & SCHEDULE DISCIPLINE

Three-Point Estimation Model

Tests best-case, most-likely, and worst-case scenarios, then calculates one weighted forecast. Visible assumptions support more realistic budgets, schedules, reserves, and decisions.

SUMMARYPLANNINGDESIGNBUILDTEST
HOW THE WEIGHTING WORKSBest case × 1 + Most likely × 4 + Worst case × 1
WEIGHT ×1BEST CASE

Effort when assumptions hold and avoidable friction is low.

WEIGHT ×4MOST LIKELY

Expected working conditions; this estimate carries four times the influence.

WEIGHT ×1WORST CASE

Effort when material risks, delays, or constraints occur.

ADD THE WEIGHTED VALUES(Best case × 1 + Most likely × 4 + Worst case × 1) ÷ 6=WEIGHTED FORECAST
THREE ESTIMATE INPUTS→ONE CALCULATED FORECASTOne forecast—not a fourth estimate—so uncertainty stays visible before budgets and schedules are committed.
